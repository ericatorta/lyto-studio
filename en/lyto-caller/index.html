What does it cost in practice?

First month, within 300 minutes: €439, including setup.

Following months, within 300 minutes: €149/month.

With 450 minutes in a month: €149 + 150 × €0.30 = €194, plus setup if it is the first month.

Additional offices, numbers and integrations: custom proposal.

No mandatory annual plan. Cancellation notice, start date and minute accounting are specified in the proposal. Any call-forwarding charges from your phone provider are excluded. VAT excluded where applicable.

Before you decide

The answers you need.

Do I need to change my number?

The planned configuration uses call forwarding. We check compatibility and any charges from your phone provider first.

What happens if it does not know an answer?

We define the information it can use and the cases to pass to the agency. The planned workflow collects a callback number without promising unconfirmed availability or terms.

Can it book appointments and use property listings?

It depends on your agency’s tools. Calendars, listings and integrations are checked and quoted in the proposal; they are not automatically included in the base plan.
