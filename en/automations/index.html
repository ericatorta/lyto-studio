Before you decide

The answers you need.

Does it always need AI?

Not always. We use simple rules and connections where they are enough. AI helps interpret requests or draft content, with checks agreed together.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

It can happen. We define which actions need approval, how to flag missing information and when to hand a request to a person. Automation does not eliminate the need for oversight.

Which tools can you connect?

We check access, APIs and tool limits during discovery. Compatibility is never assumed; external licences and costs are listed in the proposal.
